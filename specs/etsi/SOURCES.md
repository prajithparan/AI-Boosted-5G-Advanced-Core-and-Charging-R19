# ETSI material in this directory

Two kinds of ETSI material are used by the Lawful Interception work (ADR-0364), and they are
handled differently because ETSI licenses them differently.

## Schema files (committed)

ETSI publishes the machine-readable schemas of its LI specifications in a public repository under
the BSD-3-Clause licence (`LICENSE` here is that file, verbatim):
https://forge.etsi.org/rep/li/schemas-definitions

## Project-owned schema files (committed, not ETSI)

One `.xsd` in `103221-1/` is authored by this project, not fetched from ETSI, and is NOT under the
BSD-3-Clause licence above:

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `103221-1/x1-validation.xsd` | ADR-0372 validation wrapper. libxml2's schema loader needs a `schemaLocation` for every imported namespace; the normative `TS_103_221_01.xsd` imports the 103280 common namespace and the X1 HashedID namespace without one. This wrapper supplies those locations and imports the normative schema unchanged (its own `targetNamespace` is the project URN `urn:5gc-r19:li:x1-validation-wrapper`). The authoritative schema remains ETSI's `TS_103_221_01.xsd`. |

## Specification documents (NOT committed)

`TS_103_221-1_v1.23.1.pdf`/`.txt` and `TS_103_221-2_v1.10.1.pdf`/`.txt` are the ETSI
deliverables themselves. Their copyright notice reads "No part may be reproduced or utilized in
any form or by any means ... except as authorized by written permission of ETSI", which is not
compatible with committing them to a public repository, so they are gitignored. Fetch them for
offline reading with:

    python3 tools/specs/fetch_etsi_specs.py

which downloads the exact versions cited in `docs/DECISIONS.md` ADR-0364 from
https://www.etsi.org/deliver/ and converts them to text with `pdftotext` (poppler-utils).
